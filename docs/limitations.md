# Limitations

Clean Vector Reconstruction is meant for flat and semi-flat illustrations whose large regions, object boundaries, and visual relationships can be rebuilt as a manageable set of SVG shapes.

Clean colour-block compositions, geometric objects, still lifes, structured interiors, architecture, and repeated systems such as shelves, products, windows, and tiles are often good starting points. A drawing may be busy and still work well when its construction is easy to read.

Mixed scenes can work too, especially when the main structure does not depend on reproducing one particular organic contour. They usually benefit from a close Illustrator review and a little local refinement.

People with articulated limbs, hands, hair, complex animals, dense occlusion, painterly work, heavy texture, soft low-contrast edges, and dense natural detail are less predictable. In those cases, a clean editable reconstruction may demand more drawing than the workflow is meant to provide.

An SVG can parse correctly and still need work. Automated checks find structural problems; they do not decide whether the drawing feels right, whether its groups are useful in Illustrator, or whether the next change is a small adjustment instead of a redraw. That remains a human call.
